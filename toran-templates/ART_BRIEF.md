# Toran — art brief for illustrated designs

Goal: the soft, illustrated South-Indian look of the cards on Instagram / Crafty Art /
SeeMyMarriage — but made for our app, where **the app writes the text** on top.

## What creates that "vibe" (from the reference cards)
1. **Painted illustration, not graphics** — watercolour/gouache texture, soft edges, paper grain.
2. **Warm pastel ground** — peach, cream, blush, sandal; never pure white or flat red.
3. **One hero scene at the bottom 35–45%** — couple in pattu saree + pancha/kanduva,
   temple gopuram, pillared mandapam, palace steps with peacocks.
4. **Framing on the sides/top** — banana plants, mango-leaf toran, marigold & jasmine strings,
   brass bells, arches. Light touch, never crowding the middle.
5. **Big calm empty space in the upper 55–60%** — that's where the wording sits.
6. **Typography** — small tracked capitals for lines like "WE CORDIALLY INVITE YOU",
   a flowing script for the couple's names, serif for details. In the app: English font
   **"Invite"** (Cormorant SC + Pinyon Script) — added for this look.
7. **Couples are stylised** — seen from behind, side-on, or simple faces. No real person's likeness.

## Rules for every artwork
- Portrait **9:16, 1080 × 1920** (or larger, same shape).
- **No text, letters, numbers or logos in the image** — AI tools add gibberish text; reject those.
- Upper 55–60%: light, low-detail, softly textured background only.
- No watermark, signature, border cut-off, or extra limbs/fingers (check couples closely).
- Same family of colours inside one design; keep enough contrast for dark-maroon text.
- Export WebP in Template Studio (it compresses to ~150–250 KB).

## Prompt recipe
Paste one scene + the style block. If the tool supports it, set aspect ratio 9:16.

**Style block (append to every prompt):**
> soft watercolor and gouache illustration, South Indian Telugu traditional aesthetic,
> warm pastel palette of peach, cream, blush pink, turmeric yellow, leaf green and antique gold,
> delicate hand-painted details, subtle paper texture, elegant and serene, vertical 9:16 poster,
> the upper 60 percent of the image is an empty softly textured light background with no objects,
> reserved for text, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no signature

### Wedding (ceremonies: wedding, engagement)
1. **Temple walk** — a bride in a red-gold pattu saree with jasmine in her braid and a groom in a white pancha and kanduva walking hand in hand towards a South Indian temple gopuram, seen from behind, small at the bottom of the frame, banana plants on both sides.
2. **Mandapam** — a decorated wedding mandapam with carved stone pillars, marigold and mango-leaf torans hanging from the top edge, a bride and groom seated side by side under it at the bottom of the image, brass lamps and kalasam in front.
3. **Red arch** — a tall maroon temple-arch doorway with thin gold borders framing the whole page, a small bride and groom standing at the bottom centre, a lotus pond at their feet.
4. **Peacock palace** — palace steps at the bottom with two peacocks on either side, a bride in a magenta silk saree and a groom in cream holding a jasmine garland, a banyan tree branch curving in from the top right corner.
5. **Jeelakarra-bellam moment** — side view of a Telugu bride and groom seated face to face at a wedding mandapam, a thin cloth held between them, rice grains and flower petals in the air, framed by brass bells hanging from the top.
6. **Talambralu** — bride and groom showering each other with yellow rice grains at the bottom of the image, marigold strings hanging down both sides.
7. **Night reception** — a softly lit garden at dusk with string lights, the couple at the bottom, deep indigo-to-peach sky (use cream text colour with this one).

### Functions (catalog ceremonies: wedding — used on the Schedule page too)
8. **Haldi** — turmeric-yellow background with a brass bowl of haldi, marigolds and mango leaves around the bottom edge.
9. **Mehendi / Sangeet** — dholak, henna cones and marigold garlands at the bottom, soft green and pink.

### Other ceremonies
10. **Gruhapravesam** (housewarming) — a new South Indian house at the bottom with a mango-leaf toran over the door, a kalasam and rangoli at the doorstep, banana plants at the gate, morning sunlight.
11. **Satyanarayana Vratham** (satyanarayana) — a pooja setup at the bottom: kalasam with coconut and mango leaves, banana-leaf with prasadam, brass lamps glowing, marigold strings at the top corners.
12. **Barasala** (barasala) — a wooden cradle (uyyala) decorated with marigold and jasmine at the bottom, soft pastel blue and peach, small birds.
13. **Annaprasana** (annaprasana) — a silver bowl of payasam with a small spoon on a banana leaf, toys and flowers around it.
14. **Seemantham** (seemantham) — a smiling mother-to-be in a green silk saree seated with glass bangles and flowers around her, side view, at the bottom of the image.
15. **Shashtipoorthi** (shashtipoorthi) — an elderly couple in traditional attire seated with garlands, a decorated backdrop of marigolds.
16. **Upanayanam** (upanayanam) — a young boy in a dhoti with a sacred thread seated by a small sacred fire, brass vessels around, seen from the side.

| Dark (night reception) | 40, 60, 320, 380 | #FBEBC8 / #F2C14E | Telugu: NTR · English: Elegant |
If the art behind the text is busy, set the white veil to 0.3–0.5.

## Where to make the art
- **AI image tools** that allow commercial use on your plan (check the current terms):
  Adobe Firefly (built for commercial safety), Ideogram, Google Gemini, ChatGPT image generation, Leonardo.
  Never upload someone else's card as a reference to copy.
- **Illustrator** on Instagram/Fiverr/Behance: send this brief, buy full commercial rights,
  ask for layered files. Start with 10–15 designs.

## Before publishing a design
- [ ] No letters or fake text anywhere in the art
- [ ] Hands, faces and saree folds look right at full size
- [ ] Telugu preview in Studio is readable at phone size
- [ ] Ceremonies ticked correctly; version bumped if art changed
